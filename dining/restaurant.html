What people come back for

#14in town

  • काली मिर्च चिकन

    Kali Mirch Chicken

    Chicken in a black-pepper gravy. The heat comes from pepper, not chilli, and it arrives late.

  • दाल मखनी

    Dal Makhani

    Black lentils and kidney beans, slow-cooked with butter and cream.

  • मटन रोगन जोश

    Mutton Rogan Josh

    Mutton braised in a deep red gravy of Kashmiri chilli and whole spice.

  • दही कबाब

    Dahi Kabab

    Hung-curd patties, crisp outside and soft within. The vegetarian order that surprises people.

  • पनीर लबाबदार

    Paneer Lababdar

    Paneer in a rich tomato and cream gravy.
