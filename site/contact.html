Finding us

Opposite the SDM Court, on the road that goes up.

Moti Mahal Inn sits on Nainital Road at the point where Haldwani stops being a plains town and starts being the way into Kumaon. The bus and railway stations are both walking distance.

Getting here

From the bus station
Come out of the main gate and turn right onto Nainital Road. Three hundred metres — about four minutes on foot, past the SDM Court gate. If you have luggage, any auto will do it for twenty rupees and you will feel silly about it.
From Haldwani railway station
One kilometre. Turn left out of the station and stay on the main road. About twelve minutes walking, five by auto. Trains from Delhi and Lucknow come in here.
From Kathgodam railway station
Six kilometres back towards town. Autos and shared jeeps run constantly; a private auto is around ₹150. This is where the Ranikhet Express and the Shatabdi terminate — the line stops here because the hills start.
From Pantnagar Airport
Thirty-two and a half kilometres, about fifty minutes. Taxis wait outside arrivals — or ask us and we will arrange your pickup and drop.
Driving
NH-87 from Rudrapur and Rampur. We are on the left as the road begins to climb towards Nainital, opposite the SDM Court. Parking on site is free for guests.
